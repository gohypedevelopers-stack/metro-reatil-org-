Joinery FAQs

Common questions about our bespoke joinery services in Dubai.

Carpentry typically covers structural, site-based woodwork such as framing and fixing. Joinery refers to precision-crafted, factory-made wooden elements — furniture, cabinets, doors, staircases, and decorative millwork. At Halo, we provide both: our in-house joiners craft the elements, and our site team installs them.

Yes. All of our joinery is produced entirely in our own 15,000 sq.ft. factory located in Al Quoz Industrial Area, Dubai. This means full quality control, faster production times, and no third-party outsourcing costs passed on to you.

Absolutely. We regularly work alongside interior designers, architects, and developers. Simply share your shop drawings, mood boards, or concept designs and our team will produce accurate technical drawings and a detailed quote. We can also offer design-and-build if you need full support.

Timelines depend on the scope and complexity. A bespoke wardrobe or kitchen unit typically takes 3–4 weeks from approval to installation. A full villa or office joinery package can take 6–10 weeks. We provide a production schedule with every quote so you always know exactly where your project stands.

We serve residential (villas, apartments), commercial (offices, corporate fitouts), hospitality (hotels, restaurants, bars), and retail (shops, showrooms, boutiques) across Dubai and the wider UAE.

Yes. All joinery produced and installed by Halo Interiors comes with a workmanship warranty. Specific terms are included in your project contract and vary depending on material type and project scale.
